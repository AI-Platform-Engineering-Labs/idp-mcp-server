# On-call handbook
- Rotations are weekly, Monday 10:00 IST to Monday 10:00 IST, managed in **Beacon**.
- You are paged only for your own services' critical alerts. Acknowledge within 15 minutes.
- Each service's on-call contact is in the catalog (`oncall` field). Escalation: primary, then secondary after 10 minutes, then the engineering manager.
- Swap shifts in Beacon at least 48 hours ahead.
- After a night page you may start late the next day; tell your manager.
- Keep a short handover note in your team channel at the end of the week.
