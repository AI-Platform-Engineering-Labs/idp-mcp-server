# First-week checklist for new engineers
Work through this in your first five working days. Your buddy signs off on day 5.

- **Day 1:** laptop enrolled, SSO login works, you are in `#platform-help` and your team channel.
- **Day 1:** request access with `skyctl access request --team <your-team>`; approval takes up to one business day.
- **Day 2:** run the local stack (see [Developer environment setup](dev-environment-setup)) and open the portal.
- **Day 2:** read the [security baseline](security-baseline) and complete the 20-minute secure-coding quiz.
- **Day 3:** shadow one deploy with your buddy ([How to deploy and roll back](how-to-deploy-and-rollback)).
- **Day 4:** read the [on-call handbook](on-call-handbook). You will not be on call before week 8.
- **Day 5:** ship one small change to a non-critical service (tier `standard`).
