# How to request a new service
New services are created from a golden path ([Choosing a golden path](choosing-a-golden-path)).

## Steps
1. Pick the template (`python-service`, `node-service`) and your team.
2. Choose a valid name ([Naming conventions](naming-conventions)); it must not exist already.
3. Submit from the portal (Create) or ask your AI assistant to request it.
4. **A human approves** every creation request: platform on-call reviews within one business day.
5. After approval the repo, pipeline, dashboards and catalog entry appear.

Nothing is created until approval. This keeps cost, ownership and security under control.
