# Deploy freeze policy
Regular freeze: **Friday 15:00 IST to Monday 09:00 IST**.

Extra freezes: the week of the annual sale (announced in `#engineering` four weeks ahead) and the last two business days of each quarter.

Exceptions (security fixes or an active Sev 1 rollback) need the on-call engineering manager's approval, recorded in the change ticket. Rollbacks are always allowed.
